      Why do prices differ?

      The same film can cost more at a different branch, even in Regular 2D. Prices also change by screening day and studio format.

      Compare the same studio format to see the venue price difference. Premiere and VIP add comfort and service; IMAX adds a specialised screen and sound experience.

      Cinema XXI’s pricing explanation ↗

      Premiere, VIP, Executive or IMAX?

      Cinemas use different names for their seating. IMAX describes the screen, projection and sound.

      Regular or Deluxe · standard seating
      Regular at XXI and Cinépolis, and Deluxe at Denpasar Cineplex, are grouped for comparing standard tickets. Seats and facilities still vary by venue.
      Premiere · Cinema XXI ↗
      Reclining seats, a more private auditorium and butler service. Food and drinks cost extra.
      VIP · Cinépolis · photo ↗
      Reclining seats with food and drinks served by attendants. A comparable comfort-focused experience; seats, menus and service can vary by venue.
      Executive · Denpasar Cineplex · photo ↗
      Its room with wide sofa seats, above Deluxe in its seating range. This label does not promise the same recliners or service as Premiere and VIP.
      IMAX ↗
      A large, immersive screen with specialised projection and sound. IMAX 2D needs no 3D glasses.

      The “Premiere, VIP or IMAX” filter includes only those three names. Choose Executive separately, or Any format to compare everything.

      About this map

      The 12 cinemas in JadwalNonton’s Bali listing, checked 5 October 2026. This listing may not include independent screening venues.

      Pins use OpenStreetMap cinema or mall locations. Sidewalk Jimbaran’s pin is approximate. Use Directions for the venue’s location.

      Movie comparisons use a dated snapshot of JadwalNonton’s cinema schedules. Automatic refreshes are scheduled around 07:17 and 13:17 Bali time (WITA), though they may run later. Movies appear or disappear as cinemas change that day’s listings.

      Use “Sign in with GitHub” above, then “Refresh movie data” to update the listings. Only Arthur-Freedom can start a refresh. It usually takes about a minute, and the new listings load automatically. You can also run a refresh on GitHub ↗. Reloading alone loads the latest saved snapshot.

      Prices are listed per ticket; later showtimes may have different prices. Check the cinema’s booking page for the final total and seat availability.

      The Spoken language filter uses explicit film-language information from JadwalNonton, Indonesia’s LSF film guide or a verified distributor source. Dubbing and subtitles can vary by screening; unverified languages stay Unknown.

      Distances are straight-line estimates, not driving distances. Your starting point is saved only in this browser.

      New-movie notices are remembered in this browser. Personal push alerts are optional for Arthur-Freedom; the device’s push address is stored privately and is never published on GitHub. Turn alerts off above or in your browser settings. Alerts follow successful refreshes, not live cinema updates.
