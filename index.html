    Premiere, VIP or IMAX?

    Premiere and VIP are similar luxury options from different cinema chains. IMAX focuses on the screen, picture and sound.

    Premiere · Cinema XXI ↗
    Reclining seats, a more private auditorium and butler service. Food and drinks cost extra.
    VIP · Cinépolis ↗
    Reclining seats with food and drinks served by attendants. A comparable comfort-focused experience; seats, menus and service can vary by venue.
    IMAX ↗
    A large, immersive screen with specialised projection and sound. IMAX 2D needs no 3D glasses.

    For comfort, look for Premiere or VIP. For a big-screen experience, look for IMAX.

    Why do prices differ?

    The same film can cost more at a different branch, even in Regular 2D. Prices also change by screening day and studio format.

    About this map

    The 12 cinemas in JadwalNonton’s Bali listing, checked 5 October 2026. This listing may not include independent screening venues.

    Pins use OpenStreetMap cinema or mall locations. Sidewalk Jimbaran’s pin is approximate. Use Directions for the venue’s location.

    Movie comparisons use a dated snapshot of JadwalNonton’s cinema schedules. Automatic refreshes are scheduled around 07:17 and 13:17 Bali time (WITA), though they may run later. Movies appear or disappear as cinemas change that day’s listings.

    Refresh listings (owner) ↗ — sign in to the repository owner’s GitHub account and choose “Run workflow” on main. Recent refreshes took about a minute; reload this page after the run finishes. Reloading alone loads the latest saved snapshot.

    Prices are listed per ticket; later showtimes may have different prices. Check the cinema’s booking page for the final total and seat availability.

    Distances are straight-line estimates, not driving distances. Your starting point is saved only in this browser.
