## Design Context

### Users
The owner, using a simple practical map to find movie theaters in Bali and compare where to go. The creator confirmed that this is mainly for their own use.

### Brand Personality
Clear, calm, useful. Help people orient themselves and reach a theater's schedule quickly.

### Aesthetic Direction
The creator's reference is their Australia 462 specified-work map: light basemap, compact left side panel, direct search and location selection. Keep that visual structure, adapting the content to cinemas. Use restrained navy and amber accents with theater-chain colors only where meaningful.

### Design Principles
- The map and theater list are the first screen.
- Every pin corresponds to a documented cinema or its hosting mall.
- Names, addresses, and schedule links matter more than decoration.
- Search and selection work with a keyboard and touch.
- Explain the listing's coverage and data date without implying live ticket availability.
